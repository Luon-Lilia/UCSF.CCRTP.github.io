Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior lab experience to apply? +
No. CCRTP is designed for students who are new to research. What matters is curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to learn. We train you from the ground up.
Is the program paid? +
Yes. Interns receive a competitive hourly stipend for the full eight weeks, co-funded by CCRTP and MESA at Laney College. Research should not require you to work for free.
Where is the program held? +
Labs are located on UCSF campuses in the Bay Area, including Mission Bay and Parnassus, and affiliated research institutes. The program is in-person.
Can I apply if I have DACA status? +
DACA recipients are eligible to apply. Please contact us directly if you have questions about your specific situation. We are committed to working with you.
What does the program look like day-to-day? +
Most of your time is spent in the lab working on your project with your mentor. There are also weekly cohort meetings, ImmunoDiverse lectures, SRTP professional development sessions, and a final symposium where you present your research as a poster.
Will this help my transfer or medical school application? +
Yes, meaningfully. CCRTP gives you a poster presentation, a UCSF mentor who can write a strong letter of recommendation, and a biomedical research experience that stands out on any application.
