Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior lab experience to apply? +
No. CCRTP is designed for students who are new to research. We train you from the ground up. What matters is curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to learn.
Is the program paid? +
Yes. Interns receive a competitive hourly stipend for the full eight weeks. We believe research should not require you to work for free.
Where is the program held? +
Labs are located on UCSF campuses in the Bay Area (Mission Bay, Parnassus, and affiliated institutes). The program is in-person.
Can I apply if I am undocumented? +
DACA recipients are eligible to apply. Please contact us directly if you have questions about your specific situation — we are committed to working with you.
What does the program look like day-to-day? +
Most of your time is spent in the lab working on your project with your mentor. There are also weekly cohort meetings, professional development workshops, and a final symposium where you present your research as a poster.
Will this help my transfer or medical school application? +
Meaningfully, yes. CCRTP gives you a poster presentation, a UCSF mentor who can write you a strong letter of recommendation, and a research experience that stands out on any application.
