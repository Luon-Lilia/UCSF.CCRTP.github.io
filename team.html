Program founders

👤
Add photo

Vickie Bowcut

CCRTP Founder · PhD Student, Shokat Lab, UCSF
Vickie founded CCRTP through Dr. D'Anne Duncan's GRAD 210 course on JEDI Academic Leadership, drawing on her own experience as a community college and transfer student. She built the program from the ground up, recruiting labs, designing curriculum, and securing sponsorship, to give community college students access to the paid research experiences that change trajectories.
vickie.bowcut@ucsf.edu Shokat Lab
👤
Add photo

Luis Oliva

ACS SEEDlings Co-Founder · UCSF
Luis co-founded ACS SEEDlings at UCSF, the first ACS Project SEED site ever established at the institution. The program places high school students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds into paid summer research experiences at the intersection of chemistry, biochemistry, and human health.
luis.oliva@ucsf.edu Susa Lab
👤
Add photo

Sydney Figueroa

ACS SEEDlings Co-Founder · UCSF
Sydney co-founded ACS SEEDlings alongside Luis Oliva, bringing Project SEED's 50-year mission of opening science to economically disadvantaged students to UCSF for the first time. She is also affiliated with the Shokat Lab.

Dr. D'Anne Duncan

Assistant Dean for Graduate Student Success · GEPA, UCSF
Dr. Duncan oversees GEPA's student success initiatives and taught the GRAD 210 JEDI Academic Leadership course in which CCRTP was originally conceived. She provides the institutional home for CCRTP within GEPA.

Dr. Yvonne Garcia

SRTP Manager · GEPA, UCSF
Yvonne manages the Summer Research Training Program and supports CCRTP's integration into GEPA's shared professional development curriculum, including the weekly SRTP series attended by CCRTP and SEEDlings interns.
