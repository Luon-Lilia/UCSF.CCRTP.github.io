How to add a poster

  1. Put the thumbnail image (JPG or PNG) in assets/posters/
  2. Put the PDF in assets/posters/
  3. In posters.html, find id="cohort-2026" and duplicate the poster card block
  4. Fill in the intern's name, project, mentor, abstract, and file paths in the data-* attributes
  5. Add <img src="assets/posters/filename-thumb.jpg" alt="Poster"> inside .poster-thumb
