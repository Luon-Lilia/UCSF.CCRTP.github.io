Why CCRTP exists

Community college students are among the most talented and driven in California's higher education pipeline, yet they are systematically excluded from research opportunities reserved for four-year university students. CCRTP was built to close that gap: placing community college students directly into active biomedical research labs at UCSF, with real mentorship, real pay, and a clear path forward.

What the program offers

Eight weeks. Real science. A community of people who get it.

🔬

Hands-on lab research

Work shoulder-to-shoulder with graduate students, postdocs, and faculty on ongoing biomedical projects. You are not doing busy work.

💬

Dedicated mentorship

Every intern is matched with a direct mentor in the lab and has access to the PI throughout the summer.

📊

Research poster presentation

Interns present their work at a formal symposium at the end of the summer, building a credential for transfer and med school applications.

🏛️

Professional development

Workshops on graduate school, the physician-scientist pathway, personal statements, and navigating higher education as a first-gen student.

🤝

Cohort community

You are part of a group of peers going through the same thing. That network lasts beyond the summer.

💵

Paid stipend

Research should not require you to work for free. CCRTP provides a competitive hourly stipend for the full eight weeks.
