Community College Research Training Program

A paid summer research internship at UCSF for California community college students, built to open the doors of biomedical research to first-generation and underserved students.

UC San Francisco's Community College Research Training Program (CCRTP) places community college students directly into active biomedical research labs at UCSF for a paid eight-week summer internship. The program provides hands-on research experience, dedicated mentorship, professional development, and a formal research poster presentation at an end-of-summer symposium.

About CCRTP

CCRTP grew out of Dr. D'Anne Duncan's GRAD 210 course on JEDI Academic Leadership, where founder Vickie Bowcut developed a paid research opportunity for community college students as her course project. Her inspiration came from her own experience as a community college and transfer student, and from the experiences of close friends and colleagues who took the same path.

At many community colleges, research opportunities are scarce. Community colleges serve a disproportionate share of students who are first-generation, from low-income backgrounds, or from unstable home environments, and for whom income during school is essential. Once they transfer, limited research experience makes it harder to compete for paid positions, creating a cycle that restricts access to career knowledge, mentorship, and resources. CCRTP was created to break that cycle.

The program is housed under UCSF's Division of Graduate Education and Postdoctoral Affairs (GEPA) and is designed as an expansion of SRTP, the Summer Research Training Program, bringing community college students from disadvantaged backgrounds into meaningful research experiences at UCSF.

About ACS SEEDlings

ACS SEEDlings is the first ACS Project SEED site ever established at UCSF, co-founded by Luis Oliva and Sydney Figueroa. Project SEED was founded in 1968 when the American Chemical Society committed to opening the doors of chemistry and science to students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds, supporting more than 13,500 high school students across 40 U.S. states and territories since then.

At UCSF, SEEDlings places high school students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds into paid summer research experiences at the intersection of chemistry, biochemistry, and human health.

Program goals

  • 1
    Expand UCSF's outreach to institutions with fewer research resources.
  • 2
    Equip community college and high school students with the tools and experiences needed to pursue higher education.
  • 3
    Increase the diversity of future scientists by inspiring students to see themselves in research and biomedical careers.

Program features

Hands-on lab research

Work alongside graduate students, postdocs, and faculty on active UCSF biomedical projects for eight weeks. Real science, not busy work.

Paid stipend

CCRTP and MESA co-fund intern stipends. Research should not require you to work for free.

Dedicated mentorship

Every intern is matched with a direct lab mentor and has access to the PI throughout the summer.

Research poster presentation

Interns present their work at a formal end-of-summer symposium, a credential that stands out on transfer and graduate school applications.

Immunology lecture series

Weekly immunology crash course lectures and career development workshops delivered by ImmunoDiverse.

Professional development

The SRTP weekly professional development series on graduate school, the physician-scientist pathway, and navigating higher ed as a first-gen student.

CCRTP

Community College Research Training Program

For California community college students. Eight-week paid summer research internship at UCSF. Founded by Vickie Bowcut through GEPA's GRAD 210 course. Co-funded by MESA at Laney College.

ACS SEEDlings

ACS Project SEED at UCSF

For high school students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. The first ACS Project SEED site at UCSF. Co-founded by Luis Oliva and Sydney Figueroa. Sponsored nationally by the American Chemical Society.

Institutional partners and sponsors

CCRTP is built on collaboration across UCSF divisions, community college programs, and external organizations.

GEPA

Division of Graduate Education and Postdoctoral Affairs manages sponsorship funds, intern onboarding, stipend issuance, and insurance. Overseen by Dr. D'Anne Duncan and Dr. Yvonne Garcia.

MESA at Laney College

Mathematics, Engineering, Science Achievement at Laney provides outreach support and funds half of all CCRTP intern stipends. Program Director: Alyssa Arnold. Career Advisor: Shawel Getachew.

BMS DEI Committee

Student members and faculty who led sponsor recruitment, mentor outreach, application and rubric development, and curriculum design.

ImmunoDiverse

Delivers the weekly immunology crash course lecture series and career development workshops shared across CCRTP, SEEDlings, and SRTP interns.

SRTP

The Summer Research Training Program contributes the weekly professional development series attended by CCRTP and SEEDlings interns.

ACS Project SEED

National sponsor of the SEEDlings program, providing institutional support, national scholarship resources, and career pathways for high school participants.
